========================================================
PART 6 — RISK ENGINE
========================================================

Create a dedicated service:

RiskEngine

The LLM must NOT calculate the final score directly.

The engine receives validated findings.

Default project-defined severity weights:

CRITICAL = 35
HIGH = 25
MEDIUM = 15
LOW = 7

These values are DeploySafe's own scoring design.

They are NOT an industry safety standard.

Document this clearly.

========================================================
PART 7 — RISK CALCULATION
========================================================

Implement deterministic scoring.

Conceptually:

finding contribution
=
severity weight
× confidence factor
× impact/scope factor

Choose simple, explainable factors.

Example confidence:

HIGH = 1.0
MEDIUM = 0.8
LOW = 0.6

Example impact:

HIGH = 1.0
MEDIUM = 0.8
LOW = 0.6

Do not introduce excessive mathematical complexity.

Normalize final score to:

0–100

The exact formula must be:
- deterministic
- documented
- unit tested
- reproducible

Same findings must always produce the same score.
